Car engine uses real recordings. They are not synthesized tones, and the playback rate stays at 1.

Sitting in the car without moving is silent. There is no idle loop.

engine-throttle.ogg — one rev when the car starts moving (加油门).
  Source: Freesound "Car Go Fast!" by Beetlemuse.
  Page: https://freesound.org/people/Beetlemuse/sounds/678961/
  License: CC0 1.0.
  Attribution: Car Go Fast! by Beetlemuse (Freesound), CC0.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  The shipped file is the short ~2.6 second take, played once. It is not looped.
  A longer take of the same recording and an alternate rev by kyles
  (https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/450774/, CC0) were considered and not shipped.

engine-drive.ogg — while the car is actually moving, after the rev.
  Source: Freesound "Car Interior Driving Down Road" by jc144940.
  Page: https://freesound.org/people/jc144940/sounds/179121/
  License: CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; credit is included anyway.
  Attribution: Car Interior Driving Down Road by jc144940 (Freesound), CC0.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  A steady cruise from inside the car (about 6s–14s of the take), high-passed at 75 Hz so the engine and road stay audible instead of a sub-bass rumble. 7.91 second loopable mono Ogg. Not a race-car rev. Volume 0.36.

engine-horn.ogg — one honk when the child double-taps the car while seated.
  Source: Freesound "Nissan Altima car horn honk (raw)" by ChrisReierson.
  Page: https://freesound.org/people/ChrisReierson/sounds/433578/
  License: CC0 1.0.
  Attribution: Nissan Altima car horn honk by ChrisReierson (Freesound), CC0.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  About 2 seconds, played once. Not a loop. A single tap does not honk.
  Getting out is the Get out button, not a second tap.

Getting out, opening a panel, blocking chat, leaving the page, or turning SFX off stops the rev, the cruise, and a honk in progress. Stopping the car stops the rev and the cruise, so a parked car stays quiet. A honk that already started can finish while the car is parked and the child is still seated.

engine-idle.ogg is the previous Mixkit idle. The game no longer plays it.
engine.ogg is the older UX51 Mixkit "Cars starting" slice. The game no longer plays it.
