Car engine uses two real recordings. The game crossfades between them while the child is in the car. They are not synthesized tones, and the playback rate stays at 1.

engine-idle.ogg — child sitting in the car, not moving.
  Source: Mixkit royalty-free car engine idle.
  License: Mixkit Sound Effects Free License (royalty-free, attribution not required).
  https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree
  Cleaned 5 second loopable mono Ogg. Plays under the existing SFX switch.

engine-drive.ogg — while the car is actually moving.
  Source: Freesound "Car Interior Driving Down Road" by jc144940.
  Page: https://freesound.org/people/jc144940/sounds/179121/
  License: CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required; credit is included anyway.
  Attribution: Car Interior Driving Down Road by jc144940 (Freesound), CC0.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  A steady cruise from inside the car (about 6s–14s of the take), high-passed at 75 Hz so the engine and road stay audible instead of a sub-bass rumble. 7.91 second loopable mono Ogg. Not a race-car rev.

Getting out, opening a panel, blocking chat, leaving the page, or turning SFX off stops both loops immediately. Idle and drive crossfade only with each other.

engine.ogg is the previous UX51 slice of Mixkit "Cars starting" (#1561). The game no longer plays it.
engine-replace.ogg was a single-loop fallback and is not shipped. Idle and drive are separate loops.
