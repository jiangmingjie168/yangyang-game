# UX56 — cuter pig and rabbit

HUD: `build cd91b62+ux56`. Preview branch only.

The pig and the rabbit are redrawn in the same paper-ink style as the other animals: thick black outline, round shapes, flat fills.

- Pig: side-standing, round pink body, floppy ears with darker pink insides, curly tail, black cleft hooves, big eyes, blush, and a smile.
- Rabbit: side-sitting on its haunches, long ears with pink insides, blush, a small black nose, a cotton-ball tail, and paws by the chest.

`ANIMAL_SCALE` is `pig: [0.66, 0.65]` and `rabbit: [0.64, 0.66]` (was `[0.75, 0.7]` and `[0.38, 0.38]`). Both are about 0.68–0.69 of the child’s visible height. The rabbit’s headroom when a block is placed matches the pig, so the taller ears are not shoved into the block above. Tap still shows hearts and `Look! A pig!` / `Look! A rabbit!`, and the first meet still records the discovery. Cries, other animals, the car, and the boat are unchanged.
