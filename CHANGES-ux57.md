# UX57 — thinner pig and rabbit outlines

HUD: `build cd91b62+ux57`. Preview branch only.

The UX56 pig and rabbit kept the cute shapes, but the black stroke was too heavy and made the snouts, ears, and eyes look coarse.

The outline is now about half as thick (`1.72` in the drawing, was `3.35`), with lighter lines for the smile, brows, ear insides, toes, and hoof cleft. Body and head share one smooth contour instead of a double neck line. The pig still stands in profile; the rabbit still sits. Fills stay flat.

Scale is unchanged: pig `[0.66, 0.65]`, rabbit `[0.64, 0.66]`, about 0.65–0.67 of the child’s visible height. Tap still shows hearts and `Look! A pig!` / `Look! A rabbit!`, and the first meet still records the discovery.
